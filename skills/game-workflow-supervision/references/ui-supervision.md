# UI supervision

Read this only when the selected work changes visible UI, player-facing copy, art choices or formal UI readiness. Apply the matching mode and existing authority; exploratory work, functional interfaces and compatible repairs retain their own paths.

At dispatch, repair and join, compare actual visible/copy changes with the recorded [UI classification](../../game-task-planning/references/ui-adapter.md). Return new visual composition or changed meaning to its design/copy owner and retain only still-valid evidence.

For a formal UI screen, also inspect the run's `UX prerequisite` before dispatching affected art-direction decisions, new component structure, formal screen-spec, mockup, or their downstream handoff. Require an exact resolved UX decision ID/revision and separate verification, or an exact approved compatible-reuse decision with its unchanged-scope evidence. If absent, stale or contradicted by current screen content/target/interaction, record the affected consumers as blocked and route a bounded `game-ui-ux-design` task; do not promote an older mockup verdict by writing a new prerequisite after the fact. Recheck the prerequisite at join and after changes to task flow, hierarchy, feedback or dense-content layout. Keep exploratory drafts, playable functional UI and compatible pure styling/technical repairs on their documented paths without granting them formal UI readiness.

For open art-concept decisions, use [art-direction concept discovery](../../game-ui-art-direction/SKILL.md#concept-discovery). Keep the first consequential preference question ahead of unrequested production or review; use existing decisions/references and explicit delegation to avoid redundant questions. Textual directions and bounded `EXPLORATORY` samples support discussion without a formal packet or independent visual review. Do not treat a preference answer as formal visual approval. Enter formal direction only when the concept is narrowed or delegated-and-decided and the next scope is established; then apply the formal gates below. Keep necessary notes in the existing run without creating extra specialist tasks merely to conduct the conversation.

Before describing unresolved art-direction options as `VERIFIED_SELECTION_READY` or offering them for formal selection, match the author packet and separate verifier to the same current candidate fingerprint and exact decision/selection scope. Require distinct current `COMPARISON_INTEGRITY` and `VISUAL_CRAFT_READINESS` outcomes to be `PASS` for every materially distinct option, surface, state and target in that scope. Supporting parity, presence, glyph, clipping, distinctness or motion results cannot replace the craft outcome. A missing, stale, mismatched, `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` outcome keeps the affected options `DRAFT_REVISION_REQUIRED`; when none passes craft readiness, route bounded repair instead of forcing a choice. An exact selected or approved unchanged baseline may reuse still-valid evidence for a proven nonvisual-only change when authority, visual fingerprint, scope and impact proof match; do not demand new alternatives or reselection solely for that repair.

Before authorizing expensive full-screen multi-state/target art-direction expansion, or after a prior whole-screen visual-quality rejection, consume the art-direction representative preflight. Require the smallest applicable high-risk state/target set, original-detail evidence, matching candidate identity and a separate verifier. `REPRESENTATIVE_CRAFT_PREFLIGHT: PASS` authorizes only the remaining packet, never selection or the final full-scope verdict. Reuse it only when identity, scope and visual impact are unchanged. Do not impose this gate on isolated family/motion samples or an exact approved nonvisual repair. Confirm conditional visual grounding, but do not force external search or a user URL when a compatible selected/canonical authority exists or the record explains why additional references cannot change the decision.

On `ART_DIRECTION_REVISION_REQUIRED`, route only the bounded art-direction-owned repair. On `BLOCKED_BY_UPSTREAM_CONSTRAINT`, do not force a repair or selection and do not charge the art-direction retry budget while the constraint is unchanged: route player task/flow/information hierarchy to `game-ui-ux-design`; screen content/region/state/target contract to `game-ui-screen-spec`; protected font/copy/control/product policy to its product/content owner; and missing visual-thesis authority to the narrow decision owner. Record the blocked surfaces and continue unaffected independent work.

Before describing a formal mockup as `SELECTABLE`, `SELECTABLE_READY_FOR_DECISION` or otherwise offering it for formal selection, match the author packet and separate verifier to the same current candidate fingerprint and full stated selection scope. Require the verifier's distinct current `CONTRACT_COMPLIANCE` and `VISUAL_DECISION_READINESS` outcomes to be `PASS` for every materially distinct surface in that scope. A missing, stale, mismatched, `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` outcome keeps only the affected requested scope `DRAFT_NOT_SELECTABLE`; a dialog- or surface-local pass cannot promote a wider full-screen candidate. Positive visual readiness cannot be inherited from rejected, unselected, exploratory or `DRAFT` history. It may be reused from an exact selected or approved unchanged baseline only when the surface/composite fingerprint, authority, unchanged scope and explicit visual-impact proof match; do not require fresh captures, alternatives or user approval solely for a compatible nonvisual technical repair. The supervisor checks identity, scope and completeness, does not replace the visual verifier, and routes contract mismatches or missing protected visual capability to their owning UI role.
